## Section-by-Section Rationale

### Hero — REPLACE

**Why it is restaurant-branded:**
- Headline "Extraordinary Flavors, Delivered" describes a single restaurant's offering.
- Pill badge "Now open for delivery" implies a single operating restaurant.
- Primary CTA "Order Now" → `/menu` directs users to one restaurant's menu.
- Secondary CTA "Reserve a Table" implies one venue.
- Mini-stat "Loved by 50,000+ customers" reads as one restaurant's customer base.
- Background image shows a single fine-dining restaurant interior.

**New hero must convey:**
- This is a marketplace of many businesses.
- The user should search or browse to discover businesses.
- Both consumers and business owners are primary audiences.

---

### Stats — REPLACE values

**Why they are restaurant-branded:**
- "500+ Menu Items" — metric for one restaurant.
- "4.9★ Average Rating" — aggregate rating for one business.
- "30min Avg. Delivery" — delivery promise from one operator.

**New stats must convey platform scale:**
- Businesses, Branches, Cities, Customers.

---

### Categories — REPLACE values

**Why they are restaurant-branded:**
- Pizza, Burgers, Pasta, Drinks, Desserts — these are food menu categories.
- All links point to `/menu?category=*` — single restaurant menu.

**New categories must be business types:**
- Restaurant, Café, Bar, Food Court, Food Truck → `/discover?category=*`.

---

### FeaturedSection — KEEP (test constraint)

**Rationale:**  
`phase4-featured.test.ts` asserts `'<FeaturedSection />'` exists in `app/page.tsx`. The component itself is sound — it shows items from the active tenant's featured menu. On the root site it shows default tenant featured items. Repositioned below the new discovery preview sections to reduce prominence.

**Future:** P8.8.3 can repurpose this to show featured businesses rather than dishes.

---

### CTA band — REPLACE

**Why it is restaurant-branded:**
- "Reserve Your Table" — single restaurant table booking.
- "Learn More" → /restaurant — directs to the About page of one restaurant.

**New CTA must be:**
- Platform discovery CTA for consumers.
- B2B acquisition CTA for business owners.

---

### Footer — EXTEND

**Already platform-ified in P8.8.1.** P8.8.2 extends it with additional links:
- Pricing, API, Support, Documentation, Privacy, Terms.
