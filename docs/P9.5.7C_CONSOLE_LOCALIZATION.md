## Scope boundary established in this audit

The Business Console lives under `src/app/console/**` with shared shell/components under `src/components/admin/**`. Two areas that physically live under those paths are **explicitly out of scope** for P9.5.7C and were excluded from all counts below:

- `src/app/console/tenants/**` and the tenant-switching parts of the shell that manage the **platform-wide** tenant list (trial/active/suspended/churned/archived across every tenant on the SaaS) — this is Platform Administration functionality routed under `/console` for legacy reasons, not this business's own data. Belongs to P9.5.7D.
- `src/app/console/settings/configuration/**` and `src/components/admin/configuration/**` — the Configuration Studio (P9.3), explicitly named as out-of-scope. Belongs to P9.5.7D.
- `src/components/admin/platform-admin/**` — Platform Administration components. Belongs to P9.5.7D.

The Tenant/Branch **switcher UI** in the shell (`AdminTenantSwitcher`, `AdminBranchSwitcher`) was localized as part of Phase B per the explicit instruction naming "Tenant Switcher" and "Branch Switcher" as shell components — this is a small dropdown, distinct from the full platform-tenant-management page.
