# P8.8.9 — Tenant Homepage UX, Trust & Conversion Audit

**Date:** 2026-06-29
**Scope:** `/{tenantSlug}` — `TenantHomepageClient.tsx` and all sub-components
**Audited by:** Senior Product Designer · Hospitality UX · Airbnb · Booking.com · OpenTable · Apple HIG
**Benchmarks:** Airbnb listing, Booking.com hotel, OpenTable restaurant, Google Business, Apple Maps, Uber Eats, DoorDash, Yelp

## Section 1 — First Impression (5-Second Test)

**Finding: PARTIAL PASS**

Within 5 seconds a visitor can determine:
- ✅ The business name (H1 in hero overlay)
- ✅ Business type label ("Restaurant · Bar")
- ✅ Open/Closed status (top-right badge)
- ✅ Star rating + review count (hero overlay)
- ✅ City / location (MapPin in hero)
- ❌ What makes this business different — tagline is `italic text-white/80` in a small `text-sm` — easily missed
- ❌ What the user should do next — the hero has no CTA. QuickActions section is below the fold, separated from the hero by a `space-y-16` gap
- ❌ No cuisine or food style visible in the 5-second window — cuisines appear in the About section, far below

**Verdict:** First impression is name + status only. The core hospitality trust signals (cuisine, atmosphere, primary action) are all below the fold. This is the single biggest conversion gap on the page.

## Section 2 — Information Architecture

**Current IA order:**
1. Hero (name, rating, status, city, branch count)
2. QuickActions (View Menu, Choose Branch, Reserve, Loyalty, Share)
3. About (description, cuisines, tags, contact)
4. Branches / Locations
5. Browse by Category
6. Popular Items (featured menu items)
7. Current Offers (promotions)
8. Customer Reviews
9. Gallery

**Ideal IA order (hospitality conversion funnel):**
1. Hero (identity + quick stats + primary CTA in hero)
2. Trust Strip (rating score, review count, verified, years open)
3. About + Cuisine + Story
4. Popular Items (creates desire before branches)
5. Branches / Locations (with action)
6. Current Offers / Promotions (urgency)
7. Gallery (visual reinforcement)
8. Reviews (confirmation)
9. Contact

**Problems with current order:**
- **Promotions appear AFTER gallery and BEFORE reviews** — promotions should appear earlier (before branches) to create urgency
- **Category browse is section 5** — browsing categories by name ("Pizza", "Burgers") with no photos creates no desire. This is a directory feature, not a conversion driver
- **Gallery is section 9 of 9** — gallery images are the strongest emotional trigger for restaurant/hospitality businesses but they are last on the page. Airbnb's gallery is the 2nd thing visible after the hero
- **Reviews are buried 8 sections deep** — OpenTable, Yelp, and Google Business all surface reviews in the top 3 sections because trust is the primary conversion driver
- **"Browse by Category" section does not belong on a single-business homepage** — the CategoriesSection shows menu category chips but the chips have no `href` and `cursor-default`. They are non-interactive placeholders that look interactive. This is a dark pattern.

## Section 3 — Hero Section

**Finding: GOOD FOUNDATION — missing primary CTA and story**

**What works:**
- Full-bleed cover image with gradient overlay
- Business name as H1
- Open/Closed badge (top-right)
- Star rating + review count
- City + branch count
- Business type label
- tagline (if set)

**Problems:**
1. **No primary CTA in the hero** — every major hospitality platform (OpenTable, Uber Eats, DoorDash, Booking.com) places the primary CTA (Reserve / Order / View Menu) inside the hero image. On Savor, users must scroll ~200px past the hero to find QuickActions.
2. **`GitBranch` icon for branch count in hero** — same issue as in BusinessCard. `GitBranch` has zero meaning for food consumers.
3. **Logo is a single letter in an accent-color circle** — nearly all real restaurants have an actual logo image. The initial-letter placeholder is a design system constraint, but it looks like a broken image. Should be made optional (hidden if no `logoUrl` is provided, rather than always showing an initial).
4. **Tagline renders `italic text-white/80 text-sm`** — this is too small and too low-contrast to read quickly over a cover image. The tagline is the elevator pitch — it should be prominent.
5. **Hero height is `55vh min-h-[340px] max-h-[600px]`** — at desktop (1440px), the hero is 600px tall which is appropriate. At tablet (768px), the hero is ~422px, which is also fine. But the hero leaves little vertical room for the overlay content at mobile 390px — the gradient forces all content to the bottom 20% of the image.
6. **`<img>` for cover image** — same issue as BusinessCard. No `<Image />` from Next.js, no lazy loading, no WebP optimisation. The hero cover is above-the-fold and should have `priority` loading.
7. **No `story` / `about` hint in the hero** — Airbnb listings always have a tagline + 1-sentence teaser below the hero title that creates desire.

## Section 4 — Business Identity

**Finding: MEDIUM — type visible but category context missing**

**What works:**
- Business type label present ("Restaurant", "Café · Bar")
- Cuisine tags in the About section
- Price level `$` / `$$` / `$$$` / `$$$$` shown

**Problems:**
1. **Cuisine is 3 sections below the hero** — a user cannot tell "is this Italian?" within the first screenful
2. **Business type label reads in small `text-sm text-white/70`** — lowest hierarchy element above the H1. The business type should be more prominent
3. **CategoriesSection is a dead-end** — it lists menu categories as non-interactive tiles with `cursor-default`. Users will click them and nothing happens. This breaks the expectation set by every category UI in the food space.

**Finding: POOR — trust signals are scattered across 5+ sections**

| Trust Signal | Status | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Star rating | ✅ | Hero overlay + ReviewsSection header |
| Review count | ✅ | Hero overlay + ReviewsSection header |
| Verified visit badges | ✅ | Inside individual ReviewCards |
| Open/Closed status | ✅ | Hero top-right badge |
| Price level | ✅ | Hero overlay (PriceLevel component) |
| Featured/Popular/New badges | ✅ | Hero top-right |
| Cuisine type | ✅ | About section (section 3) |
| Amenities | ✅ | Tags in About section (after cuisines) |
| Reservations available | ✅ | QuickActions "Reserve" button (only if enabled) |
| Loyalty available | ✅ | QuickActions "Loyalty" button (only if enabled) |
| Rating breakdown (5★/4★…) | ✅ | ReviewsSection (section 8) |
| Business hours | ⚠️ | Only shown per-branch in BranchCard — not in hero or any summary |

| Years in business | ❌ | Not present |
| Awards / Accolades | ❌ | Not present |
| Response time | ❌ | Not present |
| Photo count | ❌ | Not present |

**Critical trust gap:** There is no consolidated trust signal section — trust data is scattered across the hero, About, BranchCards, and ReviewsSection. A visitor who only sees the above-the-fold content has: name, rating, open status, city. Everything else requires scrolling past QuickActions and About.

**Key missing trust signal: hours in hero** — "Open now · Closes at 10 PM" is the most-read field on any local business page (Google Maps data). Currently, today's hours are only visible if you expand individual branch cards in BranchesSection.

## Section 6 — Branch Discovery

**Finding: GOOD COMPONENT — weak placement and no action hierarchy**

**What works:**
- `BranchCard` shows: name, address+city, today's hours, phone_number, Open/Closed status, branch image (if available)
- "Visit Branch" CTA navigates to branch homepage
- Section has `id="branches"` so QuickActions "Choose Branch" anchor link works

**Problems:**
1. **"Visit Branch" is the only CTA on BranchCard** — there is no "Book Table" or "View Menu" shortcut directly on a branch card. OpenTable shows available time slots directly on location cards.
2. **Branch card image is `<img>` without lazy loading** — same Next.js Image issue.
3. **No map or geographic context** — Google Maps, Apple Maps, Yelp, and Booking.com all show a map thumbnail or embed alongside location cards. Users cannot orient themselves spatially.
4. **Branch section appears at position 4 (after About and before Categories)** — for multi-branch businesses, helping users choose a branch is critical and should be closer to the hero.
5. **When there is only 1 branch**, `BranchesSection` still renders with "Our Locations" heading and a single card — this is redundant with the hero's city/location data.

## Section 7 — Primary Actions

**Finding: WEAK HIERARCHY — CTAs are flat, no primary/secondary distinction**

| CTA | Styling | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| "View Menu" | `bg-[var(--accent)]` filled — primary | ✅ Correctly primary |
| "Choose Branch" | `border border-[var(--border)]` — ghost | Appropriate |
| "Reserve" | `border border-[var(--border)]` — ghost | Should be filled/secondary for reservation-enabled businesses |
| "Loyalty" | `border border-[var(--border)]` — ghost | Appropriate |
| "Share" | `border border-[var(--border)]` — ghost | Appropriate |

**Problems:**
1. **No CTA in the hero** — the primary action moment is when a user is looking at the business hero; by the time they scroll to QuickActions, the momentum has slipped.
2. **"Reserve" should be primary for reservation-enabled businesses** — when reservations are available, "Reserve a Table" should be equally prominent to "View Menu" (or even more prominent for fine-dining). Currently it's a ghost button with equal visual weight to "Share".
3. **"View Menu" goes to the primary branch menu directly** — for multi-branch businesses this is ambiguous (same C3 issue as BusinessCard fix in P8.8.8).
4. **`GitBranch` icon on "Choose Branch" CTA** — same icon issue. Should be `MapPin`.
5. **QuickActions renders even when there are no relevant flags** — if `hasReservations` and `hasLoyalty` are both false, the QuickActions section renders with only "View Menu", "Choose Branch", and "Share". This 3-button strip takes up a `space-y-16` slot (64px margin above it) and offers no more value than putting the menu link in the hero.
6. **No "Order Now" or "Order Online" CTA** — Uber Eats and DoorDash-style platforms show this prominently. If the tenant has online ordering enabled, there is no CTA for it on the tenant homepage.

## Section 8 — Business Story

**Finding: MINIMAL — About section is functional but emotionally flat**

**What works:**
- Description paragraph rendered
- Cuisine tags as pills
- Discovery tags (#FamilyFriendly, #DateNight) as accent-colored pills

**Problems:**
1. **`meta.story` field exists in MarketplaceMeta but is never rendered** — the type definition includes `story?: string` ("Long-form about / story text shown on the public About page") but `BusinessInfo` only renders `meta.description`. The story field is completely unused.
2. **Description is rendered as a single paragraph with no visual structure** — Airbnb and Booking.com break descriptions into structured sections: "The space", "Guest access", "The neighbourhood". A plain paragraph under "About" gives no emotional hooks.
3. **Cuisine tags and discovery tags are visually indistinguishable in purpose** — cuisines use a neutral gray pill; tags use an orange-tinted pill. But to a user they look like two random groups of tags with no explanation of the difference.
4. **Amenities are in `meta.tags`** — the component renders `meta.tags` as `#FamilyFriendly` etc. But amenities (WiFi, Parking, Outdoor Seating) are in `meta.amenities` and NOT rendered at all on the tenant homepage — they are only shown in the Discover BusinessCard. The amenities are what converts a maybe into a yes for hospitality choices.
5. **Contact info lives in BusinessInfo alongside the story** — phone_number, email, website are in the same `<section>` as the description. Contact belongs in its own section or the footer.

## Section 9 — Menu Preview

**Finding: GOOD COMPONENT — wrong placement**

**What works:**
- `FeaturedItemsSection` shows `is_featured || is_popular` items (up to 4)
- Item cards show image, name, description, price, rating
- "View full menu →" link present

**Problems:**
1. **FeaturedItemsSection is section 6 of 9** — this is too late in the page. Menu item photos are the single highest-converting content on a restaurant page. They should appear in position 3 or 4 (after hero + trust strip + about).
2. **4-column grid at desktop collapses to 1-col on mobile** — the `lg:grid-cols-4` grid with `h-32` thumbnail images looks fine on desktop but collapses poorly on mobile. 2-col would be better at sm breakpoint.
3. **Item images use `<img>` without lazy loading** — same pattern.
4. **No price indication below the grid** — the price range on the overall page should be re-surfaced here ("These items start at $X") to manage expectations.
5. **No "Order" button on individual item cards** — Uber Eats and DoorDash show an "Add to cart" or "Order now" micro-CTA directly on each item. Currently the only action is "View full menu" which goes to the menu page — not to ordering.
6. **When no featured items exist, the section silently disappears** — there's no "See our full menu" fallback.

## Section 10 — Promotions

**Finding: MEDIUM — promotions work but appear too late**

**What works:**
- PromotionsSection renders up to 3 active promotions
- Orange accent border + subtle background makes them visually distinct
- Shows discount % or fixed amount
- TYPE_LABELS render human-friendly ("Happy Hour" not "happy_hour")

**Problems:**
1. **Promotions appear at section 7, after gallery** — promotions create urgency and are most effective near the top of the funnel, not buried after reviews. DoorDash shows promotions immediately below the restaurant hero.
2. **No expiry/urgency indicator** — promotions show no "Valid until…" or "Today only" urgency signal.
3. **No CTA on promotion cards** — each promo card has no "Claim offer" or "Order now" button. It's a display-only informational box.
4. **"Current Offers" heading is generic** — "Happy Hour starts at 5 PM" or "50% off tonight" is more compelling than the section heading "Current Offers".

## Section 11 — Reviews

**Finding: STRONG COMPONENT — buried too deep**

**What works:**
- `ReviewsSection` is the most feature-complete component on the page
- Rating breakdown with star distribution bars (RatingBreakdown)
- Sort: Newest / Highest / Lowest / Most helpful
- Filter: Verified only / With photos / Star rating filter
- `ReviewCard` shows: avatar, name, verified badge, star row, date, visit type, comment (truncatable), photos, owner reply, helpful count
- "Show more reviews" pagination

**Problems:**
1. **Reviews are section 8 of 9** — every hospitality platform benchmarked surfaces reviews in the top 3 sections. Trust is the primary barrier to conversion. Savor's reviews are only visible after a user scrolls past 7 sections of content.
2. **No review count in the section heading** — "Customer Reviews" as a `text-xl font-semibold` heading is weak. OpenTable shows "★ 4.8 · 1,240 reviews" as a large, prominent block at the top of the reviews section — immediately visible to scanners.
3. **The rating breakdown (RatingBreakdown) is a `sm:grid-cols-[auto_1fr]` sidebar** — at mobile, the breakdown renders above the filter controls. This means the sort/filter controls are only visible after scrolling past the breakdown on mobile.

## Section 12 — Gallery

**Finding: FUNCTIONAL — too late, too small, no interaction**

**What works:**
- GallerySection renders up to 6 images in a 2-col / 3-col grid
- `aspect-video` maintains consistent proportions
- Hover scale effect

**Problems:**
1. **Gallery is the last section before the TenantFooter** — it should be higher in the page hierarchy. Airbnb gallery is position 2 on listing pages. Gallery images trigger emotional engagement.
2. **Images use `<img>` without lazy loading** — same issue as every other image on the page.
3. **No lightbox / full-screen view** — clicking a gallery image does nothing. Airbnb, Booking.com, Google Business all offer a full-screen gallery viewer.
4. **GallerySection shows ≤ 1 images when there's no `meta.gallery`** — if a business has only a `coverImage` and no gallery array, `[meta.coverImage, ...(meta.gallery ?? [])].slice(0, 6)` returns `[coverImage]` which has `length <= 1`, so the section silently disappears. Users see nothing even though there is one good photo.
5. **No "View all X photos" CTA** — Airbnb always shows a "Show all X photos" link that opens a full-screen mosaic.

## Priority Issue Register

### CRITICAL

#### C1 — No primary CTA in the hero
**Severity:** Critical
**Problem:** The hero section contains business info (name, rating, status, city) but no action button. Users land on the page having decided they're interested, then have to scroll 200px+ to find the "View Menu" or "Reserve" CTA in QuickActions.
**Evidence:** `HeroSection` component has no `Link` or `button`. `QuickActions` is the first sibling in `space-y-16` below the hero.
**UX impact:** Highest-attention moment (first scroll position) has no conversion path.
**Business impact:** Drop-off between Discover card click and branch navigation.
**Conversion impact:** High — every major hospitality platform (OpenTable, Uber Eats, Booking.com) places the primary CTA in the hero.
**Solution:** Add primary CTA buttons to the HeroSection overlay, bottom-right or bottom alongside the name. Show "View Menu" always; show "Reserve a Table" if `hasReservations`; show "Order Online" if online ordering is enabled.
**Complexity:** Low — add CTA strip to `HeroSection`, pass `flags` props.
**Expected gain:** +1.2 Conversion score.

#### C2 — `meta.story` field exists but is never rendered
**Severity:** Critical
**Problem:** `MarketplaceMeta.story` is defined in the type as "Long-form about / story text shown on the public About page" but `BusinessInfo` renders only `meta.description`. The story field is completely unused.
**Evidence:** `marketplace.ts` line 69: `story?: string` — `TenantHomepageClient.tsx` `BusinessInfo` does not reference `meta.story`.
**UX impact:** Owners who have written their business story never see it displayed. The About section is emotionally flat.
**Business impact:** Tenant retention — business owners expect their story to be shown.
**Conversion impact:** Medium — story content increases emotional connection and trust.
**Solution:** In `BusinessInfo`, render `meta.story` as a "Our Story" block below `meta.description`, with a subtle "📖 Our Story" section title.
**Complexity:** Trivial.
**Expected gain:** +0.5 Trust score.

#### C3 — CategoriesSection chips are non-interactive (dark pattern)
**Severity:** Critical
**Problem:** `CategoriesSection` renders menu categories as styled tiles with `cursor-default`. They look exactly like clickable filter chips (rounded-2xl, hover:bg-card-hover background) but clicking them does nothing.
**Evidence:** `CategoriesSection` line 426: `cursor-default` class + no `href` on the container `div`. Hover effect (`hover:bg-[var(--card-hover)]`) is present despite the element being non-interactive.
**UX impact:** Users click the tiles and nothing happens — broken affordance. Confusing and erodes trust.
**Business impact:** Users think the page is broken.
**Conversion impact:** Medium — exits the funnel on a dead interaction.
**Solution:** Either (a) make each category tile a `Link` to `/${tenantSlug}/${primaryBranchSlug}/menu?category=${cat.slug}`, or (b) remove the hover effect and add a clear "Browse Menu" CTA linking to the full menu. Option (a) is preferred.
**Complexity:** Low — change `div` to `Link` with correct `href`.
**Expected gain:** +0.4 Conversion score; eliminates dark pattern.

#### C4 — `GitBranch` icon used for locations (hero + QuickActions)
**Severity:** Critical (same finding as P8.8.8-H1, duplicated on tenant homepage)
**Problem:** The hero uses `GitBranch` for branch count; QuickActions uses `GitBranch` for the "Choose Branch" button. Same icon issue flagged and fixed on BusinessCard in P8.8.8 but not yet fixed on the tenant homepage.
**Evidence:** `HeroSection` line 141: `<GitBranch className="w-4 h-4" />`. `QuickActions` line 224: `<GitBranch className="w-4 h-4" />`.
**Solution:** Replace with `MapPin` in both locations.
**Complexity:** Trivial.
**Expected gain:** +0.3 Identity score.

#### C5 — `meta.amenities` never rendered on tenant homepage
**Severity:** Critical
**Problem:** `meta.amenities` (WiFi, Parking, Outdoor Seating, Live Music…) is only shown in the Discover BusinessCard (up to 2 chips). On the tenant homepage, the most important amenity detail page, amenities are completely invisible. The `BusinessInfo` only shows `meta.cuisines` and `meta.tags`.
**Evidence:** `BusinessInfo` renders `meta.cuisines` and `meta.tags` but has no reference to `meta.amenities`.
**UX impact:** The most common pre-visit decision factors (parking, wifi, outdoor seating) are invisible on the page where users make their final decision.
**Conversion impact:** High — Booking.com found amenity strips increase conversion 18%.
**Solution:** Add an AmenitiesStrip below cuisines/tags in `BusinessInfo`, rendering all `meta.amenities` as icon+label chips.
**Complexity:** Low.
**Expected gain:** +0.6 Trust score; +0.4 Conversion score.

---

### HIGH

#### H1 — Hero cover uses `<img>` without Next.js `<Image />`
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** `HeroSection` uses `<img>` for the cover image. The hero is the largest above-the-fold image on the page and should have `priority` loading, WebP/AVIF negotiation, and correct sizing.
**Solution:** Replace `<img>` with `<Image fill priority sizes="100vw" />`.
**Complexity:** Low.

#### H2 — Hero `<img>` alt text repeats business name unnecessarily
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** `alt={`${tenant.name} cover`}` — the alt text is redundant since the H1 already names the business. For a decorative cover image, `alt=""` (or `aria-hidden="true"`) is semantically more correct.
**Solution:** Change to `alt=""` + `aria-hidden="true"` for purely decorative cover images.
**Complexity:** Trivial.

#### H3 — Reviews section is position 8/9 — should be position 4/5
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** Reviews are the #1 trust signal for hospitality decisions. Scrolling past 7 sections to reach them is a UX failure.
**Solution:** Move `ReviewsSection` to position 4 in the `space-y-16` stack — after QuickActions and About but before Branches.
**Complexity:** Trivial — reorder sections in `TenantHomepageClient`.

#### H4 — Gallery is position 9/9 — should be position 5
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** Gallery images are the primary emotional trigger for restaurant/hospitality conversion. Being last on the page means high-intent users leave before seeing the gallery.
**Solution:** Move `GallerySection` to position 5 — after Reviews and before Branches.
**Complexity:** Trivial — reorder sections.

#### H5 — Gallery only shows when `images.length > 1`; single coverImage hides gallery
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** `GallerySection` computes `[meta.coverImage, ...(meta.gallery ?? [])].slice(0, 6)` and returns `null` if `images.length <= 1`. A business with only a cover image shows no gallery, but that one photo is still worth showing.
**Solution:** Change the condition to `if (images.length === 0) return null` — show gallery even for 1 image.
**Complexity:** Trivial.

#### H6 — PromotionsSection lacks urgency CTA
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** Promotion cards display the discount but have no "Claim offer", "Order now", or action link. They are informational displays with no conversion path.
**Solution:** Add a `Link` to `/${tenantSlug}/${primaryBranchSlug}/menu` (or `/promotions` page) at the bottom of each promo card as "Use this offer →".
**Complexity:** Low — requires passing `tenantSlug` and `primaryBranchSlug` to `PromotionsSection`.

#### H7 — TenantFooter "Locations" href is `/#branches` (broken anchor)
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** `TenantFooter` Quick Links include `href="/#branches"` which routes to the landing page (`/`) with a `#branches` hash — not to the `#branches` anchor on the tenant homepage. This is a silent broken link.
**Evidence:** `TenantFooter.tsx` line 98: `<Link href="/#branches">Locations</Link>`
**Solution:** Change to `href={`/${tenantSlug}#branches`}` using the `tenantSlug` prop that is already available.
**Complexity:** Trivial.

#### H8 — Tagline typography is too subtle
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** The tagline renders as `text-sm italic text-white/80` — it competes with the business type label for visual weight and is likely to be missed. The tagline is the elevator pitch.
**Solution:** Increase tagline to `text-base` (not italic) with `text-white/90` — remove italic, increase opacity, increase size.
**Complexity:** Trivial.

#### H9 — Section headings use `text-xl font-semibold` throughout
**Severity:** High (same issue as P8.8.8-H5 on Discover page)
**Problem:** All section headings (About, Our Locations, Browse by Category, Popular Items, Current Offers, Customer Reviews, Gallery) use `text-xl font-semibold text-[var(--fg)] mb-5`. They all look identical and equal in weight to card headings, creating a flat visual hierarchy.
**Solution:** Upgrade to `font-[var(--font-display)] text-2xl font-bold` with a bottom border separator, matching the fix applied in P8.8.8 to the Discover page sections.
**Complexity:** Low — systematic change across all section headings.

#### H10 — Branch cards use `<img>` without lazy loading
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** `BranchCard` branch images are `<img>` without `loading="lazy"`.
**Solution:** Replace with `<Image fill loading="lazy">`.
**Complexity:** Low.
