## 2. Competitive Analysis

### 2.1 Shopify Customers

Shopify's customer detail page is the gold standard for e-commerce CRM. It shows:
- **Header card**: Avatar, name, total orders, total spent, location, tags, opt-in status
- **Timeline**: every order, note, email, and event in reverse-chronological order
- **Contact info**: email, phone_number, address
- **Orders list**: with status badges, order amounts, date
- **Addresses**: primary and additional
- **Notes**: staff notes visible only internally
- **Tax info**: exemptions
- **Marketing**: consent status per channel

**Key takeaways for P8.9.7:**
- Single page, all data visible without page navigation
- Timeline is the primary interaction surface
- Staff notes prominently placed
- Tags visible on header card

### 2.2 HubSpot CRM (Contact Record)

HubSpot provides the most comprehensive single-view CRM record:
- **Sidebar**: Contact properties, company association, deals, tickets
- **Activity timeline**: emails sent/received, calls logged, notes, tasks, meetings
- **Properties panel**: custom properties, lead source, lifecycle stage
- **Associations**: companies, deals, tickets, conversations
- **Marketing emails**: sent, open/click rates
- **Social profiles**: LinkedIn, Twitter

**Key takeaways for P8.9.7:**
- Timeline is the centrepiece; every interaction is logged
- Properties sidebar for quick-glance data
- Marketing engagement metrics clearly shown

### 2.3 Square (Customer Directory)

Square's customer profiles focus on point-of-sale context:
- **Overview**: Total visits, total spend, average spend, last visit date
- **Visit history**: date, location, items purchased, amount
- **Loyalty**: points balance, tier, transaction history
- **Feedback**: reviews and ratings given
- **Groups**: customer segments/tags
- **Notes**: staff notes with timestamps
- **Marketing**: email/SMS opt-in

**Key takeaways for P8.9.7:**
- Visit history is the centrepiece (equivalent to our "timeline")
- Loyalty is prominently featured alongside visit context
- Groups = tags for segmentation

### 2.4 Toast POS (Guest Profiles)

Toast focuses on the dining context:
- **Guest info**: name, contact, allergen alerts, seating preferences
- **Visit history**: ordered by date, party size, server, revenue
- **Loyalty**: points, rewards, redemption history
- **Reservations**: upcoming and past
- **Notes**: server/host notes visible to FOH staff
- **Spend metrics**: total revenue, avg check, avg party size, frequency

**Key takeaways for P8.9.7:**
- Allergen and preference notes are critical for hospitality
- Reservation history is as important as order history
- Avg party size and seating preference are unique dining metrics

### 2.5 Stripe Customer Portal

Stripe focuses on subscription and payment context:
- **Header**: Customer ID, email, created date
- **Subscriptions**: current plan, status, next invoice
- **Payment methods**: saved cards, bank accounts
- **Invoices**: full billing history with amounts, status, PDF links
- **Charges**: individual payment events
- **Disputes**: chargeback status

**Key takeaways for P8.9.7:**
- Payment history is prominently shown
- Each payment has full metadata (method, status, amount)
- Strong audit trail for financial events

## 4. P8.9.7 Design Decisions

### 4.1 Tab architecture
10 tabs via `useState` — no URL routing between tabs, single page load. Reason: avoids full page remount on tab switch, keeps the customer profile header always visible.

### 4.2 Timeline as canonical event log
The `CustomerTimelineEvent` covers 28 event types from orders to loyalty to marketing to admin actions. This mirrors the HubSpot/Shopify model where the timeline IS the audit trail.

### 4.3 Notes design
Notes are tenant-scoped and never visible to the customer. Each note carries `authorId` and `authorName` for accountability. Inline editing (no modal) to reduce friction.

### 4.4 Tags as flexible segmentation
Tags are defined at the tenant level (not per-branch) and assigned per `(userId, tenantId)`. This allows consistent segmentation across branches within one tenant.

### 4.5 Analytics as derived data
`CustomerAnalytics` is read-only computed data — LTV, favourite items, revenue trends. Not editable by admins. In production this would be computed by background jobs or materialized views.

### 4.6 Marketing as preference + engagement
`CustomerMarketing` combines channel subscription state with campaign engagement history. Gives admins a clear view of reach and engagement without exposing campaign tools in this page.

### 4.7 Tenant isolation
All CRM data is scoped per `(userId, tenantId)`. Alice (pu-001) at Savor has completely separate notes, tags, analytics, and marketing data from Alice at Brew Café.
