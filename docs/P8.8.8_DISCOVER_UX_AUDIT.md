## Section 1 — First Impression (3-Second Test)

**Finding: FAIL — marketplace intent is not instantly clear**

Within 3 seconds a first-time visitor can determine:
- ✅ There is a search bar
- ✅ There are business type filter chips (visible after load)
- ❌ There is no count of businesses on the platform ("500+ businesses" appears only on the landing page hero, not here)
- ❌ There is no visual proof — no photos are visible above the fold without scrolling
- ❌ The hero section ("Find Your Next Favourite Spot") is visually similar to a generic search product, not a hospitality marketplace
- ❌ No social proof in the hero: no "X businesses" pill, no ratings summary
- ❌ The hero has no immediate calls-to-action other than search — no "Browse All" or popular category shortcuts below the search bar
- ❌ The gradient background (`from-orange-50 via-white to-orange-50`) reads as generic marketing page, not a rich marketplace

**Benchmark comparison:**
- **Airbnb Discover/Stays**: Shows photo-filled cards above the fold immediately. First impression is photographic and spatial.
- **Uber Eats**: Hero contains restaurant photos within 300ms of page load. No white space between CTA and inventory.
- **OpenTable**: The discover hero immediately shows restaurant photography and surfaces popular filters.

**Verdict:** The hero section costs ~2 scroll events before any business inventory is visible. Critical.

## Section 3 — Filter UX

**Finding: GOOD BASELINE — but filter panel is hidden by default**

| Filter | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Categories (type chips) | ✅ | Rendered as pill chips; always visible |
| Cuisine | ✅ | In advanced panel |
| Price level | ✅ | In advanced panel ($–$$$$) |
| City | ✅ | In advanced panel (only shown when >1 city) |
| Services | ✅ | Reservations, Loyalty in panel |
| Amenities | ✅ | In advanced panel (WiFi, Parking, etc.) |
| Open Now | ✅ | Toggle in advanced panel |
| Delivery / Takeaway | ❌ | Not present |
| Rating filter | ❌ | Not present — users cannot filter 4.5+ stars |
| Distance | ❌ | Not present (geolocation not implemented) |
| Business type | ✅ | Category chips cover this |

**Critical UX problems:**
1. **Filter panel is closed by default** — the "Filters" button is placed at the extreme right end of the chip row, and `activeCount` starts at 0, so the button has no visual prominence. Competitors (Airbnb, Booking.com) always show filters above the fold in a visible strip.
2. **Rating filter missing** — "4.5+ stars" is the #1 filter across every comparable marketplace.
3. **"Open Now" buried** — Open Now is the 2nd most common single-tap filter (after category) on Uber Eats and DoorDash, but it is hidden inside the closed panel.
4. **No filter count next to category chips** — users do not know how many businesses exist per category before clicking.
5. **Panel layout is inline (push-down)** — when opened, the filter panel pushes content down. Airbnb, Google Maps, and Booking.com use a slide-out drawer or popover so content stays in place.

## Section 5 — Business Cards

**Finding: GOOD — high information density; a few trust gaps**

**What works:**
- Cover photo with hover zoom
- Open/Closed status badge (top-left, high contrast)
- Status badge: Featured / New / Popular (top-right)
- Logo initial with brand color
- Business name + type label
- Star rating + review count
- City + branch count
- Description (line-clamp-2)
- Cuisine tags (up to 3)
- Service badges: Reservations (blue) / Loyalty (purple)
- Amenity chips (up to 2)
- CTA: "View Menu" + "Branches" / "Visit"
- Branch expand panel (BranchPreview)

**Problems:**
1. **Primary CTA "View Menu" goes directly to `/slug/primaryBranchSlug/menu`** — skips the business homepage (`/slug`). Users selecting a multi-branch business have no idea which branch they're entering. Airbnb always takes users to the listing page, not directly to availability.
2. **"View Menu" is not the right CTA for all business types** — a Bar or Hotel calling its CTA "View Menu" is a category mistake. It should be "Explore" or "Visit" for non-restaurant businesses, or at minimum "See More".
3. **No estimated delivery/wait time** — Uber Eats and DoorDash show "15–25 min" on every card. Not applicable for all types, but for food trucks and restaurants this is a key differentiator.
4. **"Branches" button with ChevronDown is not obviously a branch selector** — users don't know it will expand an inline list; the label "Branches" with an arrow looks like a navigation item. OpenTable uses "3 locations" as a distinct pill that opens a map.
5. **`GitBranch` icon for branch count** — a Git icon has no meaning to a food consumer. Should be `MapPin` or a location icon.
6. **Logo is an initial letter** — most tenants will not have custom logos, so the `accentColor` initial is the only visual identity. This is fine, but the color circle sits beside the cover photo and at 40×40px is barely visible.
7. **Card height varies** — cards with many amenity chips are taller than cards with none, causing grid row misalignment at xl breakpoint.

## Section 6 — Collections

**Finding: POOR — collections are data-driven but editorially empty**

| Collection | Source Logic | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Featured | `meta.featured === true` | Only appears if businesses are tagged; if none tagged, section disappears silently |
| Popular Right Now | `meta.popular && !meta.featured` | Same |
| Newly Opened | `meta.isNew` | Same |
| Top Rated | `meta.rating >= 4.5` sorted | Data-gated |
| Family Friendly | `meta.tags.includes('Family Friendly')` | Tag match only |
| Coffee Shops | `businessTypes.includes('cafe' or 'mobile_cafe')` | |
| All Businesses | All tenants | Always visible |

**Problems:**
1. **On an empty or partially-populated platform, all or most sections disappear** — a new-platform visitor sees "All Businesses" with 1–3 cards and no context. There is no "coming soon" or placeholder. Impression: the platform is empty.
2. **Sections do not have "View all →" links** — Airbnb's browse sections always have "Show more" / "View all". Each collection is a cul-de-sac.
3. **Section sort is independent of the SortBar** — the SortBar in discovery mode claims to control the view, but the Featured/Popular/Top Rated sections ignore the selected sort. Only "All Businesses" at the bottom is sorted.
4. **No editorial collections** — "Best for Date Night", "Great for Groups", "New This Week", "Late Night" are editorial collections that all major marketplaces (Uber Eats, DoorDash, OpenTable) use to drive discovery. Currently the only editorial tags are the `tags` array in MarketplaceMeta.
5. **Section heading font is `text-xl font-semibold`** — same weight as body card titles. Poor typographic hierarchy; sections do not feel like a structured editorial feed.
6. **Collections repeat data** — a business tagged `featured` AND `popular` only appears in Featured (because `popular.filter` excludes featured). This is good logic but not communicated to the user ("Featured" businesses include "Popular" ones).

## Section 7 — Visual Hierarchy

**Finding: MEDIUM — good component-level design, weak page-level hierarchy**

**Problems:**
1. **Hero section and body section share the same vertical rhythm** — there is no clear visual separation between the hero (search context) and the results (inventory). The `border-b` on the hero and `py-10` on the body are the only separators.
2. **Category chips and "Filters" button are on the same `flex-wrap` row** — on tablet the filters button can wrap to a second line or crowd the chips, making the filter affordance less discoverable.
3. **Section headings use the same `text-xl font-semibold` as card `h3` elements** — creates hierarchy ambiguity between section labels and card labels.
4. **`All Businesses` section is the final section but contains the most cards** — on long pages, users who scroll past the curated sections to reach "All Businesses" have no visual landmark that they've exited the editorial zone and entered the complete catalogue.
5. **Empty sections disappear silently** — if Featured, Popular, Newly Opened are all empty, the page jumps directly from the sort bar to "Top Rated" or another section with no explanation.
6. **Grid uses `xl:grid-cols-4`** — at 1440px, 4 cards per row with 5px gap is too dense; card descriptions are clipped after 2 lines and category tags are truncated.

## Section 10 — Marketplace Positioning

**Finding: WEAK — feels like a directory with sorting, not a hospitality marketplace**

**Problems:**
1. **Hero copy is aspirational but generic** — "Find Your Next Favourite Spot" could be a tagline for any local search product (Google Maps, Foursquare, Yelp). It does not communicate platform value: multi-business ordering, reservations, loyalty.
2. **Collections are labelled generically** — "Featured", "Popular Right Now", "Top Rated" are the same sections on every local listing site since 2005. OpenTable uses "Top Picks Near You", Uber Eats uses "Popular Near You" with restaurant photos in scrollable carousels.
3. **No horizontal scroll carousels** — every major marketplace (Airbnb, Uber Eats, DoorDash, OpenTable) uses horizontal carousels for curated collections on mobile. The current grid always uses vertical scroll with no horizontal differentiation between sections.
4. **No platform-level value proposition inside Discover** — once a user navigates from the landing page to Discover, there is no reminder that Samoon enables ordering, booking, and loyalty. The hero could surface "Book a table · Order online · Earn rewards" as supporting text.
5. **No map view** — Google Maps, Apple Maps, Yelp, and even Booking.com offer a map/list toggle for location-based discovery. The absence of any spatial context removes a key discovery mode.

## Section 14 — Performance UX

**Finding: MEDIUM — perceived performance is degraded by image loading strategy**

**Problems:**
1. **`<img>` instead of `<Image />` from Next.js** — no automatic format negotiation, no srcset, no blur placeholder, no priority hinting. On a 4-card row with 8+ businesses loaded, this can cause significant network waterfall on first paint.
2. **All images load simultaneously** — no `loading="lazy"` on below-fold cards. On a page with 20+ businesses, this fires 20+ image requests on page load.
3. **Cover images have no dimensions specified** — causes layout shift (CLS impact) when images load.
4. **No blur placeholder** — the cover image `<div>` is `bg-[var(--bg-secondary)]` while loading, which is fine, but no progressive blur-to-sharp transition.

## Priority Issue Register

### CRITICAL (must fix before Phase C completes)

#### C1 — Hero has no visible inventory above the fold
**Severity:** Critical
**Problem:** The entire discover hero takes up ~400px vertical space (gradient background, h1, subtext, search bar) before a single business card is visible. Users must scroll to see what the marketplace contains.
**Evidence:** Hero `py-16` + `h1 text-5xl` + `p text-lg mb-8` + `BusinessSearch max-w-2xl` = ~380px before grid begins.
**Business impact:** High bounce rate on Discover; first impression does not communicate inventory.
**UX impact:** 3-second test fails. Users cannot answer "What's on this platform?" without scrolling.
**Conversion impact:** Every scroll is a conversion opportunity lost.
**Solution:** Reduce hero vertical padding (`py-16` → `py-10`); reduce h1 size (`text-5xl sm:text-5xl` → `text-3xl sm:text-4xl`); shrink subtext margin (`mb-8` → `mb-6`). This brings the first row of business cards approximately 150px higher.
**Complexity:** Low — CSS padding/size changes only.
**Expected improvement:** +0.8 Discovery UX score.

#### C2 — "Open Now" filter is buried in a closed panel
**Severity:** Critical
**Problem:** "Open Now" is the most-used single-tap filter in every food marketplace (Uber Eats, DoorDash, Google Maps, Yelp). It is currently inside the `AdvancedFilters` panel which is closed by default and positioned at the far right of the filter bar.
**Evidence:** `DiscoverClientPage` renders `<AdvancedFilters>` at `ml-auto` — visually pushed to the right, with the panel closed. `openNow` is a boolean toggle inside the panel.
**Business impact:** Users who want to see what's open now cannot discover this filter; they may leave the platform assuming all businesses are closed.
**UX impact:** The most actionable, time-sensitive filter is invisible.
**Conversion impact:** High — Open Now filtering directly correlates with purchase intent.
**Solution:** Add a permanent "Open Now" chip to the `CategoryFilter` row (or immediately after it), which toggles `filters.openNow` directly without opening the panel.
**Complexity:** Low — add one chip button to the filter row.
**Expected improvement:** +0.7 Filter UX score; +0.5 Conversion score.

#### C3 — Business card primary CTA "View Menu" bypasses business homepage
**Severity:** Critical
**Problem:** `viewHref = /${tenant.slug}/${meta.primaryBranchSlug}/menu` sends users directly to a specific branch's menu, skipping the tenant homepage and branch selector. For a business with 3 branches, the user has no idea which branch they've been deposited into.
**Evidence:** `BusinessCard.tsx` line 46: `const viewHref = \`/${tenant.slug}/${meta.primaryBranchSlug}/menu\``
**Business impact:** Users may abandon mid-journey when they find themselves at the wrong branch.
**UX impact:** Removes agency; forces single-branch selection without explanation.
**Conversion impact:** Multi-branch businesses lose conversion because users don't trust they're at the right location.
**Solution:** Change `viewHref` to `/${tenant.slug}` (tenant homepage) for multi-branch businesses. For single-branch businesses, keep the existing direct menu link. Update CTA label to "Explore" for non-restaurant types.
**Complexity:** Low — single logic branch in BusinessCard.
**Expected improvement:** +0.6 Conversion score; +0.5 Trust score.

#### C4 — Search query and filters are not URL-persistent
**Severity:** Critical
**Problem:** After a user searches or applies filters on Discover, the URL does not update. Sharing a filtered URL, using the browser Back button, or refreshing the page loses all state.
**Evidence:** `DiscoverClientPage` uses `useState` for `rawQuery`, `searchQuery`, `selectedCategory`, `filters`, `sortBy` — none of these update `window.location` or `router.push`.
**Business impact:** Users cannot share curated results; email/social referral links always land on unfiltered Discover.
**UX impact:** Back button returns to unfiltered state — frustrating for multi-step browsing sessions.
**Conversion impact:** High — shareable filtered URLs are a growth mechanism (OpenTable allows "Italian restaurants near me" deep links).
**Solution:** Use `useRouter` + `useSearchParams` to sync `q`, `category`, `sort`, and key filter params to the URL. `useEffect` on params to initialise state on load. This makes the URL the source of truth.
**Complexity:** Medium — requires `useSearchParams` plumbing across the client page and filter components.
**Expected improvement:** +0.8 Navigation score; +0.5 Conversion score.

### HIGH

#### H1 — `GitBranch` icon for branch count is semantically wrong
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** The branch count in the stats row of every BusinessCard uses `<GitBranch className="w-3.5 h-3.5" />`. This is a version-control icon. Food marketplace consumers have no mental model for this icon.
**Solution:** Replace with `<MapPin />` or `<Building2 />` — a location icon communicates "branches = locations".
**Complexity:** Trivial.

#### H2 — SortBar uses native `<select>` — inconsistent with design system
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** Every other interactive element in Discover uses pill chips, rounded-2xl buttons, or card containers from the design system. The sort control is a native OS `<select>` dropdown with `rounded-lg` that looks out of place.
**Solution:** Replace with a custom `<select>`-like chip row (pill buttons like CategoryFilter) for the 7 sort options, or at minimum style the `<select>` to match (`rounded-2xl border-[var(--border)]`).
**Complexity:** Low — style change only; or medium if implementing custom pill sort.

#### H3 — SortBar in discovery mode misrepresents its scope
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** In discovery (non-filtered) mode, the SortBar appears above curated sections and shows "X businesses on the platform". Selecting "Highest Rated" only re-sorts the "All Businesses" section at the bottom of the page — the Featured/Popular/Top Rated sections above it are unaffected.
**Solution:** Hide the SortBar in discovery (non-filtered) mode entirely, or clearly label it "Sort all businesses ↓". In filtered mode it is correct and should remain.
**Complexity:** Low — conditional render based on `isFiltering`.

#### H4 — Image loading: `<img>` should be `<Image />` with `loading="lazy"`
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** `BusinessCard.tsx` uses `<img>` (eslint comment to suppress the Next.js warning). All card images load immediately on page render. On a page with 20+ businesses this is 20+ full-size image downloads at once.
**Solution:** Replace `<img>` with Next.js `<Image />` (fill + sizes). Add `loading="lazy"` for below-fold cards. The primary card (e.g. first 4 on the page) should use `priority`.
**Complexity:** Low — swap `<img>` to `<Image />` with fill/sizes; add `priority` prop only to first N results.

#### H5 — Section headings lack visual hierarchy vs. card headings
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** Section headings use `text-xl font-semibold` — identical weight and colour to card `h3` names. There is no visual separation between the editorial section label and the individual card titles.
**Solution:** Increase section headings to `text-2xl font-bold` with a `font-[var(--font-display)]` family, and add a bottom border or subtle divider above each section heading.
**Complexity:** Trivial.

#### H6 — Category filter chips have no business count
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** The category chips (Restaurant, Café, Bar, etc.) do not show how many businesses match each type. Users cannot make an informed choice about which category to filter by.
**Benchmark:** Airbnb always shows "(42)" next to each category.
**Solution:** Compute per-category counts from `allWithMeta` and render them as `(N)` next to each chip label.
**Complexity:** Low — add useMemo count derivation in DiscoverClientPage; pass counts to CategoryFilter.

#### H7 — Hero platform capability text missing
**Severity:** High
**Problem:** The Discover hero says "Browse restaurants, cafés, bars, food trucks and more — all on one platform." This does not remind users of the platform's key differentiators: reservations, online ordering, and loyalty.
**Solution:** Replace or supplement the subtext with "Search · Book a table · Order online · Earn rewards" as a compact capabilities strip below the heading, mirroring the hero pill added in P8.8.7.3.
**Complexity:** Trivial — copy change.

### MEDIUM

#### M1 — No "View all →" links on collections
**Severity:** Medium
**Problem:** Each collection section (Featured, Popular, Top Rated…) ends after its grid with no way to "see all". If there are 8 Featured businesses, only 4 (xl grid) are visible before the next section.
**Solution:** Add "View all →" that applies the corresponding sort/filter and switches to filtered view.
**Complexity:** Medium — requires wiring section CTA to update state.

#### M2 — Image error / broken cover fallback
**Severity:** Medium
**Problem:** If `meta.coverImage` returns a 404 or empty string, the card image area shows the raw `bg-[var(--bg-secondary)]` fill with no fallback icon or message.
**Solution:** Add `onError` handler to set a fallback gradient or placeholder.
**Complexity:** Low.

#### M3 — Empty section disappears silently
**Severity:** Medium
**Problem:** If all editorial sections (Featured, Popular, Newest, Top Rated…) are empty, the discovery view jumps immediately to the SortBar and "All Businesses" with no explanation of the empty sections.
**Solution:** When all sections are empty and businesses exist, show a "Browse everything" prompt that directs users to scroll down to "All Businesses".
**Complexity:** Low.

#### M4 — "Filters" button is far-right, easy to miss
**Severity:** Medium
**Problem:** The "Filters" button is placed at `ml-auto` in the chip row, right-aligned against the viewport edge. On desktop with many category chips, the button is 800px+ away from the left-aligned chips. This creates a wide visual gap.
**Solution:** Move the Filters button to immediately follow the category chips (not ml-auto), separated by a subtle `|` divider.
**Complexity:** Low — remove `ml-auto` from the wrapper div.

#### M5 — Card variable height causes misaligned grid rows
**Severity:** Medium
**Problem:** Cards with many amenity chips (WiFi, Parking, Outdoor Seating, Live Music) are 20–40px taller than cards with no amenities. At `xl:grid-cols-4`, this causes jagged row baselines.
**Solution:** Use CSS `grid-rows` with auto-row height or add a `min-h` on the card amenity section to absorb variable content.
**Complexity:** Low.

### LOW

#### L1 — "Branches" CTA label is ambiguous
**Severity:** Low
**Problem:** The branch expand button is labelled "Branches" with a chevron. "Branches" reads as a noun (the restaurant has branches), not as an action (click to see branches).
**Solution:** Change label to "Locations" or "See Locations".
**Complexity:** Trivial.

#### L2 — Price range uses symbols only
**Severity:** Low
**Problem:** PriceDots renders `$`, `$$`, `$$$`, `$$$$` without a text label. These symbols are meaningful to Western audiences but may not be universally understood.
**Solution:** `aria-label` already exists (Budget/Moderate/Upscale/Fine Dining). Consider adding a visible label text on hover tooltip.
**Complexity:** Trivial.

#### L3 — Skeleton count is always 6
**Severity:** Low
**Problem:** The loading skeleton always shows 6 cards regardless of the expected count. If a user is re-filtering and expects 2 results, showing 6 skeletons is misleading.
**Solution:** Pass an estimated `expectedCount` prop to `LoadingSkeleton`, defaulting to 6.
**Complexity:** Low.

#### L4 — Sort bar shows in discovery mode above collections that it doesn't sort
**Severity:** Low (partially addressed by H3)
**Problem:** Sort bar copy "X businesses on the platform" suggests sorting applies globally. It doesn't.

#### L5 — No hero platform count pill
**Severity:** Low
**Problem:** The landing page hero has a pill showing "Search · Book · Order — one platform". The Discover hero has no equivalent trust signal. A "500+ businesses listed" pill in the discover hero would reinforce platform scale.
**Solution:** Add a small platform count pill in the hero below the headline.
**Complexity:** Trivial.
